# Brainrot Wave - Game Design Overview

## Core Loop
- Survive waves while collecting brainrots for money.
- Use money to buy speed, upgrade base access, and unlock mutations.
- Rebirth to trade speed for a higher multiplier, enabling faster progression on future runs.

## Player States
- **Alive**: Can collect brainrots, enter holes, and move freely.
- **Dead**: Triggered when a wave hits the player outside a safe hole. Player respawns after a short delay.
- **Safe**: Inside a hole during a wave; immune to wave damage.

## Areas & Brainrot Tiers
Each area contains brainrots of a specific tier and a safety hole:
- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic
- Unique
- Secret
- Special

Unlock areas by upgrading the base.

## Waves
- **Standard Waves**: Travel across the map; touching them kills the player.
- **Dodge Waves**: Variants with gaps or timing windows to encourage movement skill.
- **Wave Schedule**: Regular wave intervals with randomized type rotation.

## Safe Holes
- Each area includes at least one hole.
- Standing inside a hole during a wave provides immunity.

## Money & Economy
- Collecting brainrots grants money.
- **Speed Shop**: Spend money to increase movement speed.
- **Base Upgrades**: Unlock more areas and tier access.

## Mutations
- Mutations multiply money earned per brainrot.
- Obtained via events (UFO event) and possibly rare drops.

## Rebirth
- Rebirth converts your current multiplier to a higher one, but reduces speed.
- Example cost curve for rebirths (speed thresholds): 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90...
- Rebirth multiplier gain: +0.5x per rebirth.

## Lucky Blocks
- Spawn every 15 minutes.
- Lucky block tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Unique, Secret, Special.
- Lucky blocks drop rewards (money, mutations, boosts, or cosmetics).

## UFO Event
- Spawns every 30 minutes.
- Grants mutations and has a chance to spawn UFO Lucky Blocks.
- UFO Lucky Blocks can drop higher-tier rewards.

## Progression Summary
1. Farm brainrots in the current area.
2. Buy speed and base upgrades.
3. Unlock new areas and higher-tier brainrots.
4. Use events for mutations and bonuses.
5. Rebirth to scale long-term progression.
